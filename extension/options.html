Settings. Everything is saved automatically and stays in this browser.

API key

OpenRouter key

Bring your own key. It is stored locally and only ever sent to openrouter.ai. Get one at openrouter.ai/keys.

Model

Which model writes

Any OpenRouter model id. Default is a fast, cheap one.

Your voice

About you and your tone

Added to every request. For example: "I'm a product manager at Northwind. Keep it casual and warm."

Modes

Comment styles

Edit the built-in prompts, hide ones you do not use, or add your own. Order here is the order in the popup.
